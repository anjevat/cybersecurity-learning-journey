# Common Cybersecurity Threats

## Overview

Cybersecurity threats are activities or events that can compromise the confidentiality, integrity, or availability of information and systems.

## Common Threats

- **Phishing** — Attempts to trick users into revealing sensitive information or performing unsafe actions.
- **Malware** — Malicious software designed to damage, disrupt, spy on, or gain unauthorized access to systems.
- **Ransomware** — Malware that encrypts or blocks access to data and demands payment.
- **DDoS** — Attempts to overwhelm a service with large amounts of traffic, making it difficult to access.
- **Brute Force Attack** — Repeated attempts to guess passwords or other credentials.
- **Social Engineering** — Manipulating people into revealing information or performing actions that compromise security.

## Security Relevance

Understanding common threats helps cybersecurity professionals identify risks, apply appropriate defenses, and investigate security incidents.

## Key Point

Effective cybersecurity requires understanding both the technical and human aspects of security.


## Countermeasures

- **Phishing:** User awareness training, email filtering, and multi-factor authentication (MFA).
- **Malware:** Endpoint protection, regular updates, and safe software practices.
- **Ransomware:** Regular backups, endpoint protection, patching, and network segmentation.
- **DDoS:** Traffic filtering, rate limiting, and DDoS protection services.
- **Brute Force:** Strong passwords, MFA, account lockout policies, and rate limiting.
- **Social Engineering:** Security awareness training, verification procedures, and least-privilege access.

## Key Point

Effective cybersecurity requires both understanding threats and applying appropriate preventive and defensive measures.
